About StudyHub

StudyHub is a student-focused educational platform created to make exam preparation simple, organized, and accessible.

StudyHub provides useful study resources for MP Board Class 10 and 12 students in one place.

What We Provide

📚 Notes

Subject and chapter-wise study material to help students understand and revise their topics.

📝 Mock Tests

Practice tests to improve preparation and build confidence for examinations.

📑 Sample Papers

Original practice papers based on the relevant syllabus and examination pattern.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to provide students with useful study resources in one place so they can learn, practice, and improve their exam preparation.

Who Is StudyHub For?

StudyHub is designed for students preparing for MP Board Class 10 and Class 12 examinations.
